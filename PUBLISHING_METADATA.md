## 🛡️ 5. Google Play Policy Compliance & Permission Declaration

When submitting to Google Play Console:

| Permission | Purpose / Justification |
|---|---|
| `INTERNET` | To fetch media metadata, download video/audio files from supported platforms, and sync with Telegram Bot. |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | To detect active Wi-Fi / Mobile connection and handle automatic network reconnects. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | To keep large video & audio downloads running smoothly without being killed by the Android OS. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | To show active download progress bar, speed, pause/resume buttons, and download complete alerts. |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | To prevent CPU sleep during active high-speed media downloads. |
| `Storage Access` | Uses modern Android MediaStore & Scoped Storage APIs (Zero invasive broad storage permissions requested). |
